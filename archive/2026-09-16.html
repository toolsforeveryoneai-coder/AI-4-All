The apps stay free. The AI on top now has a price

Edition of 2026-09-16

# AI For All ### Wednesday, September 16, 2026

As of today, a creator or a small business that already sells through Instagram has to decide whether to pay a monthly bundle price to keep more AI generation and more automated replies to customers, because the apps stay free and the capacity on top of them is now a product.

The free app, and the price on top of it

Meta opened a subscription called Meta One inside Instagram, Facebook, WhatsApp and Meta AI. It comes in three families: extras for a single app, bundles for individuals that add more generative use, and bundles for creators and businesses that add professional tools plus more automated replies from the company's customer agent on WhatsApp.

Meta is careful to say the free experience is not changing, and that part is true. What changed is where the limits sit. The extra image generation, the video generation from its Muse models, and the agent that answers customers while you sleep were features of the product. They are now tiers of it, with published prices starting at *$7.99 a month for the individual bundle and $14.99 a month* for the creator and business bundles.

The detail worth holding on to is the size of the launch. *More than 50 features* went live across four apps on day one, and the company says the same subscription will extend into its editing app, its AI glasses and more.

Two reserves. The page states that pricing and availability may vary by region, by app and by account, so treat those numbers as a floor rather than a quote for your country. And a subscription that meters AI usage is only as clear as the limits behind each tier, which the announcement does not publish.

Two voices shipped into the API, and one of them thinks while it talks

Google DeepMind released two models for spoken conversation. One is built for cost and scale, the other for hard tasks, and the second does something genuinely new in use: it reasons and speaks at the same time, holding the line with short verbal cues while it works in the background instead of going quiet.

Both went to developers today in the Gemini API and Google AI Studio, which means a company building a phone agent can try them now rather than joining a list. The cheaper one is also in Search Live for everyone, and the reasoning one is in private preview for enterprises.

The concrete part is the language handling. It detects and switches between *97 supported languages inside a single conversation, without being told which one comes next. The strongest evidence on the page is not the vendor's own: a first place on a voice quality index run by an independent measurement firm. The page also reports 97.7%* on an audio benchmark of Google's choosing.

That last clause is the reserve. The high scores sit on benchmarks the vendor picked, the price is called competitive without a number, and any buyer comparing voice vendors still has to ask for the rate card. One line on the page is more useful than any score: every piece of audio these products generate carries a watermark, so a clip made by a machine can be detected as one.

Read the announcement at Google DeepMind.

Salesforce stopped renting its reasoning

Salesforce announced Koa, its first reasoning model, built for Agentforce, the platform where its customers assemble their own agents. The important word is built. Instead of routing multi step work out to a frontier lab, the company post trained an open weight Nvidia model, keeps the weights, and runs it inside its own walls so no customer data crosses to a third party.

For a procurement lead buying customer service agents, that is a real fork in the road: a vendor that now competes with the model providers it used to depend on, on a licence it does not have to rent, with a data residency argument the labs cannot match. The model was trained on synthetic scenarios modelled on *27 years of the company's own customer relationship data, across more than 14 industries*, with no customer data used. That is the part a bank or a hospital asks about first.

There is no path to buy it today. Koa is in pilot with a handful of named customers, and wider availability is expected later, in US regions first. The reserve is the benchmark: the claim that it matches or beats the leaders is Salesforce's own, on tasks Salesforce selected. Test it in a pilot rather than banking on it.

Read the announcement at Salesforce.

Weak signal

The backlash against American data centres is being counted wrong, according to SemiAnalysis, which mapped the local restrictions and the state actions and concluded the buildout is not being legislated to a halt. Its finding is a matter of scale: the capacity genuinely delayed by local moratoriums and New York's executive order is small next to the computing capacity the country is expected to add next year, once every restriction is matched to a real project.

It stays below the bar because it changes no rule and gives nobody anything to do today, and because the numbers come from a model the same firm sells to its clients. It is still the counterweight worth carrying, because the opposite version of this story is told every week.

Watch the tiers, not the features. The price of AI capacity inside products you already pay for is now the number that moves.
